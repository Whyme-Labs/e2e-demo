What we are building

Claim Interlock turns a vague stalled state into a typed blocker: the unresolved fact, the responsible institution, the minimum sufficient evidence, and the action boundary that must not be crossed.

Utility

Blocker intelligence

Diagnoses why the next valid state transition cannot occur, rather than summarising the entire case again.

Architecture

Institutional agents

Insurer, workshop and adjuster agents retain independent identities, private data, tools and legal authority.

Safety

Deterministic action gate

Gemini may interpret and propose. A non-LLM policy layer authorises case-, resource- and action-scoped mutations.

Privacy

Minimum disclosure

Requests the smallest attestation needed for the current blocker instead of copying every underlying document.

Reliability

Durable execution

Event ledger, idempotency keys and action receipts allow recovery without duplicated notices, tasks or approvals.

Control

Human authority

Coverage denial, settlement, payment and repair authorisation remain explicitly gated by an authorised reviewer.

The four-minute proof

The submission is designed around observable state change—not a generic agent conversation or a claims dashboard.

What the experiments actually found

The pack preserves adverse evidence. Large efficiency gains did not justify autonomous progression when diagnosis quality was insufficient.

Supported

Internal navigator first

Most simulated handling-time improvement came from exact blocker diagnosis before any federated network effect.

Conditional

Federation is not always faster

Its robust benefits are minimum disclosure, separation of authority and data sovereignty. Speed depends on endpoint reliability and acceptance.

Kill criterion

False-ready risk

At 84% modeled blocker recall, the system improved effort and latency but regressed safety. The MVP is therefore governed and human-supervised.

Explicit non-claim: this prototype does not autonomously deny coverage, determine fraud, set settlement amounts, approve payment or replace professional motor-loss assessment.
